# Session 10.2 — Power Icons & Shared Plumbing

## Goal

Generate all 10 power icons, populate the registry with real entries, and build the shared infrastructure every individual power (sessions 10.2.1–10.2.10) will need. This session ships no power *behavior* itself — it's the foundation the ten power-specific sessions build on.

## Depends on

Session 10 (`PowerBase`, `NoOpPower`, `registry.js`, hook call sites, `mismatchStreak`, `cardMismatchCounts`), session 10.1 (selector UI consuming `POWERS`), session 5.2 (`Timer`, `ScoreController`), session 5.4 (match/explode animation and lock timing), session 4 (`Board`), session 3 (`Card`).

## Icons

- `src/assets/powerIcons.js`, mirroring session 2's `cardArt.js` approach: draw each icon procedurally on an off-screen canvas (gradients/shapes, no external image files), output as a `dataURL` (`canvas.toDataURL()`) or reusable `<canvas>` element — these render in the DOM carousel (10.1), not the three.js scene.
- One glyph per power, badge-style (circular background in a themed color + a simple centered icon shape), ~128×128:
  - **Telepathy** — purple background, concentric rings or a stylized eye.
  - **Time Traveler** — teal/blue background, hourglass or clock-with-arrow.
  - **Luck of the Draw** — green background, four-leaf clover or a die face.
  - **Cold as Ice** — ice-blue background, snowflake.
  - **Touch of Death** — dark red/black background, skull or skeletal hand.
  - **Greasy Fingers** — amber/yellow background, fingerprint or smudge swirl.
  - **Battery Powered** — bright yellow background, a battery shape with a lightning bolt through it.
  - **X-ray Vision** — cyan/white background, an eye glyph overlaid with crosshatch/skeletal lines.
  - **Electric Monkey** — orange background, a simple monkey-face silhouette (circle face + two small ear circles) with a lightning bolt overlay.
  - **Sleight of Hand** — dark navy/black background, a stylized glove/hand silhouette with a playing-card corner peeking from behind it.
- `generatePowerIcon(id)` → returns the drawn asset; cache like session 2 did (generate once, reuse).

## Registry

Populate `POWERS` in `src/game/powers/registry.js` with 10 entries: `{ id, name, description, icon }`. Description copy (kept short enough for the 10.1 carousel):

- Telepathy — "After 3 mismatches, your next flip reveals its match with a purple glow — but that match is worth half points."
- Time Traveler — "Every match adds 5 seconds to the clock. 3 mismatches in a row costs you 2 seconds."
- Luck of the Draw — "Random flips sometimes auto-match. 5 mismatches in a row deals 2 fresh pairs onto the board."
- Cold as Ice — "Every 3rd move freezes a random card face-up — only one at a time. Left unmatched, another card locks face-down every 3 moves after that, until any match breaks the ice."
- Touch of Death — "Mismatch the same card twice and it auto-matches. Do it 3 times total, or twice in a row, and you instantly lose."
- Greasy Fingers — "Matching a pair sometimes flips another card for a 1-second peek — but not twice without a match in between."
- Battery Powered — "Tap a face-up card again to supercharge it. Match it for double points, mismatch it and lose 50 points."
- X-ray Vision — "2 charges. Flash the whole board face-up for half a second. Push your luck too far afterward and you'll burn a pair and lose time — or worse."
- Electric Monkey — "Tap a face-up card twice to charge it up. Matching next zaps a random pair for half points and +10 seconds — mismatching paralyzes 2 random cards."
- Sleight of Hand — "2 charges. Secretly fix your next mismatch into a match. The card you swapped out gets marked — match it later for double points, mismatch it and you're caught."

Leave each power's actual class as `NoOpPower` (from session 10) until its dedicated session lands — the registry entry existing with real copy/icon doesn't require the behavior to exist yet, so this session is safe to ship standalone and playtest the selector fully before any power does anything.

## Shared plumbing (used by 10.2.1–10.2.6)

- **Score multiplier hook**: `ScoreController.recordMatch(secondsRemaining, options)` gains an optional `{ multiplier: 0.5 }`-style param (default 1), applied to both base points and streak bonus. (Needed by Telepathy.)
- **Timer adjustment hook**: `Timer.addSeconds(n)` (positive or negative), clamped so it can't push the clock negative or (if you want a sane ceiling) above some max like 5:00. (Needed by Time Traveler.)
- **Board pair-injection hook**: `Board.addPairs(n)` — pulls `n` unused face ids from the 32-pool if available for the current level (or reuses ones already in play if the pool is exhausted, e.g. on hard mode), spawns the new face-down pairs into the layout, and re-flows/re-scales the grid (reuse session 4's `layout()` sizing logic) without disturbing already-matched (removed) or currently-frozen cards' positions any more than necessary. (Needed by Luck of the Draw — budget extra time here, it's the trickiest new piece of infra in the whole powers arc.)
- **Frozen card state**: `Card` gains a `frozen` flag; when true, the card stays face-up and is excluded from the normal mismatch-flip-back timer, but remains clickable/targetable for matching. `Board`/`GameController` must skip auto-hiding frozen cards during mismatch resolution. (Needed by Cold as Ice.)
- **Forced-loss path**: `GameController` gains a way to immediately end the game as a loss (distinct from the session 5.2 timeout path) — locks input, sets final score to 0, and routes to the same game-over/leaderboard flow a timeout uses. Session 7's win/game-over screen should treat "loss" generically (a `won: false` result with a reason) rather than assuming timeout is the only non-win ending. (Needed by Touch of Death, Sleight of Hand.)

None of Time Traveler, Luck of the Draw, Cold as Ice, or Touch of Death's specific *logic* is implemented here — only the shared primitives they'll call.

## Shared plumbing (used by 10.2.7–10.2.10)

- **Repeat-tap arm hook**: `onRepeatTapFirstPick(card, ctx)` — a new `PowerBase` hook fired when the player taps the currently-selected first-pick card again while it's still face-up, unmatched, and awaiting a second pick (i.e., before a second card has been chosen and before any mismatch lock/animation begins). Generalizes the "tap the face-up card again to arm something" gesture needed by both Battery Powered and Electric Monkey, so neither reimplements tap-detection from scratch.
- **Generic card tag/highlight**: extend `Card` with a generic `setHighlight(color, durationMs)` (durations optional/`Infinity` for "until cleared") instead of a Telepathy-specific purple-only highlight — Telepathy (purple), Sleight of Hand (red "marked" card), and any future power can all reuse this one mechanism with different colors. Also add a generic `armedTag` string field (e.g. `'supercharged'`, `'electric'`) so a power can flag its own card state without inventing a new boolean per power.
- **Temporary lock-for-N-moves**: `Card.paralyzedForMoves` (integer countdown), separate from the permanent-until-matched `iceLocked` state Cold as Ice already uses — decrements by 1 on every `onTurnResolved`, card is unselectable while `> 0`, automatically clears (and becomes selectable again) at 0. (Needed by Electric Monkey.)
- **Board burn-pair hook**: `Board.burnRandomPair({ scoreMultiplier = 0 } = {})` — picks a random still-unmatched, unlocked, non-frozen pair remaining on the board, removes both cards from play (counts toward the win-condition's remaining-pairs total the same as a normal match would, so the game can still be completed), and awards `scoreMultiplier × the normal points that pair would've earned` (`0` = pure penalty/no points, `0.5` = half points). (Needed by X-ray Vision, Electric Monkey.)
- **Score deduction hook**: `ScoreController.deductPoints(n)` — subtracts a flat number of points from the running score, clamped at a floor of 0. (Needed by Battery Powered, X-ray Vision.)
- **Charge-based HUD button**: `src/ui/PowerChargeButton.js` — a reusable button component showing remaining charges (e.g. "2/2"), disabled once at 0, invoking a power-supplied `onActivate()` and decrementing its own charge count. (Needed by X-ray Vision, Sleight of Hand.)
- **Full-board peek**: `Board.peekAll(durationMs)` — flips every still-face-down, unmatched, non-frozen card face-up for `durationMs`, then flips them all back down automatically (any card already face-up, matched, or frozen is left untouched). (Needed by X-ray Vision.)

None of Battery Powered, X-ray Vision, Electric Monkey, or Sleight of Hand's specific *logic* is implemented here — only the shared primitives they'll call, same pattern as the first plumbing pass above.

## Done when

- All 10 icons render distinctly and legibly in the 10.1 carousel, each with correct name/description copy.
- Selecting any power still plays a completely normal game (since each is still `NoOpPower` under the hood) — confirms the registry/copy work is decoupled from behavior.
- `ScoreController.recordMatch(secondsRemaining, { multiplier: 0.5 })` halves points correctly in isolation (unit-test or console-verify); `ScoreController.deductPoints(50)` correctly subtracts and clamps at 0.
- `Timer.addSeconds(5)` and `Timer.addSeconds(-3)` correctly adjust the visible countdown and respect clamping.
- `Board.addPairs(2)` correctly injects 2 new face-down pairs into a live board and re-lays-out the grid without displacing matched/removed cards, tested on at least one level size.
- `Board.burnRandomPair({ scoreMultiplier: 0.5 })` correctly removes a random unmatched pair, counts it toward win completion, and awards half its normal points; `{ scoreMultiplier: 0 }` awards none.
- `Board.peekAll(500)` correctly flips all eligible cards face-up for ~500ms and back down without disturbing matched/frozen/already-face-up cards.
- Setting `card.frozen = true` on a face-down-flip-then-mismatch card correctly leaves it face-up and un-flippable-back during a manual test; `card.paralyzedForMoves = 3` correctly blocks selection for exactly 3 turns then clears automatically.
- `card.setHighlight('red', Infinity)` and `card.setHighlight('purple', 3000)` both render correctly and the timed variant clears itself.
- Tapping an already-face-up, unmatched first-pick card fires `onRepeatTapFirstPick` exactly once per arm (doesn't double-fire on rapid taps).
- A manually triggered forced-loss call correctly locks input, zeroes the score, and reaches the game-over/leaderboard flow.
- A `PowerChargeButton` with 2 charges correctly disables itself after 2 activations and re-enables on a fresh game.
