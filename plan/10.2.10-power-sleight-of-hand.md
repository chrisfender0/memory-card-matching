# Session 10.2.10 — Power: Sleight of Hand

## Goal

Implement Sleight of Hand: a 2-charge HUD button that secretly rescues your next mismatch into a match — but marks the swapped-out card as a trap that can pay off big or end the game.

## Depends on

Session 10 (`PowerBase`, hook call sites), session 10.2 (registry entry + icon, `Card.setHighlight`, `ScoreController.recordMatch` multiplier param, `PowerChargeButton`, the shared forced-loss path).

## Spec

- **Button**: mount a `PowerChargeButton` (session 10.2) in the HUD with 2 charges. Activating it arms a one-shot flag, e.g. `rescueArmed = true` — nothing happens immediately; it waits for the next mismatch.
- **Rescue (consumes the arm on the next mismatch after activation)**: when the next mismatch occurs while `rescueArmed`:
  1. Identify the two mismatched cards: the first-pick card (`correctAnchor`) and the wrong second-pick card (`wrongCard`).
  2. Instead of the normal mismatch resolution, flip `wrongCard` back face-down (as it would've gone anyway), and automatically reveal `correctAnchor`'s real partner elsewhere on the board, completing a match between them (normal match flow: animation, full-value scoring — no discount for this rescued match itself).
  3. Mark `wrongCard` as the "trap" card: `wrongCard.setHighlight('red', Infinity)` (visibly red on the board, per spec — this is an intentionally visible tell, not a hidden trap) and track it as `markedCard` for future turns.
  4. Consume the charge and clear `rescueArmed`.
- **Trap resolution (future turns involving the marked card)**:
  - If a later turn flips `markedCard` and it results in a **match**: award **double points** for that match (`ScoreController.recordMatch(secondsRemaining, { multiplier: 2 })`), then clear the mark (`markedCard = null`, remove the highlight — the card is gone from the board now anyway since it matched).
  - If a later turn flips `markedCard` and it results in a **mismatch**: the player is "caught" — immediately trigger the shared forced-loss path (lock input, score forced to 0, route to game-over/leaderboard flow same as Touch of Death's instant-loss).
  - If `markedCard` is never touched again, the mark simply persists (no time limit) until the game ends or it's eventually engaged one way or the other — implement it this way (persistent trap) rather than expiring after exactly one turn, since a one-turn-only window is both hard to make fair and easy to trivially dodge by just not touching that card once.
- Using the second charge while a `markedCard` from the first use is still unresolved is allowed — it arms a second rescue independently; when it fires, its own `wrongCard` becomes the (new) marked card. Decide simply: if two marks end up live at once, either card triggers the same match/mismatch consequences independently (keep the logic per-card, don't special-case "two marks at once" beyond making sure both are tracked, e.g. as a `Set` of marked cards rather than a single variable).

## Files touched

`src/game/powers/SleightOfHand.js` (new, extends `PowerBase`), `src/game/powers/registry.js` (point Sleight of Hand's entry at the real class), `src/ui/HUD.js` or equivalent (mount the `PowerChargeButton`).

## Done when

- Selecting Sleight of Hand: the HUD button shows 2/2 charges; activating it arms the rescue with no immediate visible effect.
- The next mismatch after activation is silently converted into a match (correct partner auto-revealed, wrong card flipped back down), and the wrong card is now visibly highlighted red on the board.
- Later flipping the red-marked card into a match awards exactly double points for that match.
- Later flipping the red-marked card into a mismatch immediately ends the game with score forced to 0.
- Using both charges across a game correctly tracks both resulting marked cards independently if both are still unresolved at once.
- The button disables itself at 0 charges remaining.
- Playing with a different power selected (or powers off) shows zero Sleight of Hand behavior.
