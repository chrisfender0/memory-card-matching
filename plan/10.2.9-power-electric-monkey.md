# Session 10.2.9 — Power: Electric Monkey

## Goal

Implement Electric Monkey: tapping your face-up first-pick card twice charges it electrically — a supercharged match zaps a random other pair for bonus time, a supercharged mismatch paralyzes two random cards.

## Depends on

Session 10 (`PowerBase`, hook call sites), session 10.2 (registry entry + icon, the `onRepeatTapFirstPick` hook, `Card.armedTag`, `Card.paralyzedForMoves`, `Board.burnRandomPair`, `Timer.addSeconds`).

## Spec

- **Arming**: same repeat-tap gesture as Battery Powered (session 10.2.7) — tapping the current first-pick card again while it's face-up and unmatched fires `onRepeatTapFirstPick(card, ctx)`. Set `card.armedTag = 'electric'` with a distinct visual (sparking/electric outline, different from Battery Powered's supercharge glow so the two aren't visually confused if a player somehow compares them across games).
- **Resolution — match**: if the turn resolves as a match and the first-pick card had `armedTag === 'electric'`:
  - Award the normal match points for the pair actually completed (no multiplier on this pair itself).
  - Additionally call `Board.burnRandomPair({ scoreMultiplier: 0.5 })` — picks a *different* random unmatched pair still on the board and force-matches/removes it for half its normal points ("lightning burns a random pair of cards for 50% pts").
  - Additionally call `Timer.addSeconds(10)`.
  - Guard: if no other unmatched pair remains on the board to burn (e.g. this was the last pair), skip the burn silently — still award the timer bonus.
- **Resolution — mismatch**: if the turn resolves as a mismatch and the first-pick card had `armedTag === 'electric'`: pick 2 random other still-face-down, unmatched, unlocked cards and set `card.paralyzedForMoves = <N>` on each (pick a reasonable fixed duration, e.g. 3–5 turns, and keep it as a named constant) — they become unselectable until the countdown clears on its own (per the shared `paralyzedForMoves` mechanism from session 10.2, no special early-clear rule needed unless you want matches to clear it early, which is optional/your call).
- Clear `armedTag` once the turn resolves either way (charge doesn't carry over to the next turn).

## Files touched

`src/game/powers/ElectricMonkey.js` (new, extends `PowerBase`), `src/game/powers/registry.js` (point Electric Monkey's entry at the real class).

## Done when

- Selecting Electric Monkey and playing: tapping the current face-up first-pick card a second time visibly charges it electrically (distinct from Battery Powered's supercharge visual).
- Completing a match with the first-pick card charged awards normal points for that pair, burns a different random unmatched pair for half its points, and adds 10 seconds to the clock.
- Completing a mismatch with the first-pick card charged paralyzes 2 random other cards, making them unselectable for the configured duration, then automatically clearing.
- Turns where the first-pick card was never charged behave with completely normal scoring/mismatch handling.
- Playing with a different power selected (or powers off) shows zero Electric Monkey behavior.
